# Governance

Bedriox is developed and maintained by the Veno Ninja LLC team. Veno Ninja LLC controls official repositories, releases, infrastructure, signing keys, and project trademarks.

Technical decisions are documented through RFCs. Maintainers evaluate contributions on correctness, security, compatibility, tests, maintainability, licensing, and project scope. Contributors retain copyright in their contributions, which are submitted under GPL-3.0-only unless clearly stated otherwise.
